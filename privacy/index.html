Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

LockIn is built to hold you accountable — not to collect your data. This policy explains exactly what we do and don't do with information on your device.

Overview

LockIn stores your habit data locally on your device and, if cloud sync is active, in a private Firebase Firestore database tied to an anonymous account. We do not collect personal information, do not run ads, and do not sell data — ever.

What we collect

  • Habit data — habit names, icons, log history, streaks, and notes you create.
  • Your name — only if you choose to enter it in the app. Stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous device ID — Firebase assigns a random identifier used to sync your data across devices. It cannot be linked to you personally.
  • App preferences — theme, reminder times, accessibility settings. Stored locally.
  • Push notification tokens — only if you grant notification permission, and only to deliver reminders you set up.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or camera access
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry sent to third parties

How your data is used

Your habit data is used solely to display streaks, statistics, and history within the app. Cloud sync data is stored under your anonymous Firebase UID. It is not accessible to anyone else — including us.

Third-party services

The only third-party service LockIn uses is Google Firebase (Firestore + anonymous authentication) for optional cloud sync. Firebase's own privacy policy governs their handling of infrastructure-level data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We use no advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or social login providers.

How to delete your data

Open LockIn and go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete all data. This permanently erases all habits, logs, streaks, and notes from both your device and the cloud. The action cannot be undone.

Children

LockIn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will ensure their data is deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
