About

Built for people who are serious about getting better.

LockIn was built because most habit apps are designed to make you feel good about showing up halfway. They reward streaks you didn't earn, give you badges for skipping, and turn accountability into a game you can cheat. LockIn doesn't do any of that.

⚡
Discipline over motivation
Motivation fades. Systems don't. LockIn is built around showing up every day — not just when you feel like it.
🎯
Focus over quantity
You can track up to 10 habits in Pro — but we'll nudge you toward 3–5. Research is clear: tracking fewer habits builds stronger routines.
📊
Honesty over dopamine
Your heatmap and daily score don't lie. LockIn shows you the raw truth about your consistency — no inflation, no participation trophies.
🔒
Privacy over data
No account required. No ads. No data sold. Your habits stay on your device and in a private anonymous cloud you control.

The story

LockIn started as a personal project after getting frustrated with habit apps that feel more like games than tools. Too many features, too much celebration for mediocre effort, and zero real accountability.

The goal was simple: build the app that would actually hold you to your word. No streaks you can cheat with freezes unless you've earned them (Pro only, once a week). No "good job for trying" when you missed. Just an honest record of what you did and didn't do.

LockIn is built and maintained by an indie developer. It's a one-person app, which means every feature is intentional and every piece of feedback matters.
